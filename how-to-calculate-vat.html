How to Calculate VAT — Step by Step with Examples

📅 February 2025 · ⏱ 4 min read · ✍️ VATCalc Team

Calculating VAT correctly is essential for any business or freelancer issuing invoices. Whether you need to add VAT to a price, remove it, or find the VAT component within a total, the maths is simple once you understand the formulas.

The Three VAT Calculations You Need

1. Adding VAT to a Net Price

Use this when you know the price before VAT and want to find the total price the customer pays.

VAT Amount = Net Price × VAT Rate
Total Price = Net Price + VAT Amount

— OR in one step —

Total Price = Net Price × (1 + VAT Rate)

Example at 20% VAT: Net price is €250.
VAT Amount = €250 × 0.20 = €50
Total (inc. VAT) = €250 + €50 = €300

Example at 5% VAT: Net price is £80.
Total = £80 × 1.05 = £84

2. Removing VAT from a Gross Price

Use this when you have a price including VAT and want to find the net price. This is sometimes called "backing out" or "reverse calculating" VAT.

Net Price = Gross Price ÷ (1 + VAT Rate)
VAT Amount = Gross Price − Net Price

Example at 21% VAT: Total price is €363.
Net Price = €363 ÷ 1.21 = €300
VAT Amount = €363 − €300 = €63

⚠️ Common mistake: Don't simply multiply by 20% to find the VAT in a price that includes VAT. That gives the wrong answer. Always divide by (1 + rate).

3. Finding the VAT Rate from Two Known Prices

VAT Rate = (Gross ÷ Net) − 1

Example: Net price €200, gross price €240.
Rate = (€240 ÷ €200) − 1 = 1.20 − 1 = 0.20 = 20%

VAT Calculation with Reduced Rates

Many items have reduced VAT rates. Always check the correct rate for your specific product and country. For example:

  • Food in Germany: 7% (not 19%)
  • Books in France: 5.5% (not 20%)
  • Medicine in Spain: 4% (not 21%)
  • Energy in the UK: 5% (not 20%)
