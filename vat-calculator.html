Frequently Asked Questions
Everything you need to know about VAT calculation
VAT (Value Added Tax) is a consumption tax applied at each stage of production and sale. To add VAT: multiply your net price by (1 + VAT rate). For example, €100 at 20% VAT = €100 × 1.20 = €120. To remove VAT from a gross price: divide by (1 + VAT rate). So €120 ÷ 1.20 = €100 net.
B2C (Business to Consumer): You charge VAT to the end customer. B2B (Business to Business): Within the EU, if both parties are VAT-registered, the reverse charge mechanism may apply — the buyer accounts for VAT instead of the seller. This eliminates the need for foreign sellers to register for VAT in the buyer's country.
Most countries apply reduced VAT rates to essential goods like basic food, medicine, and children's products to make them more affordable. For example, in Spain, basic food has a 4% super-reduced rate, while restaurants charge 10%, and most other goods are taxed at 21%.
This calculator uses standard published VAT rates for each country. Rates are updated regularly.
