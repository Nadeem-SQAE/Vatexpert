About Us

About VATCalc

VATCalc is a free online VAT, GST and Sales Tax calculator designed to help businesses, freelancers and individuals calculate tax correctly across 150+ countries. We built VATCalc because we were frustrated by existing tools — they were either too simple (showing only one rate per country) or too expensive (requiring subscriptions for basic functionality).

Our mission is simple: make correct tax calculation free and accessible to everyone in the world.

What We Do

VATCalc provides accurate VAT, GST and Sales Tax rates for 150+ countries, updated regularly to reflect legislative changes. Unlike basic percentage calculators, we include:

  • Every reduced rate and zero rate per country
  • Product category filtering (food, medicine, electronics etc.)
  • B2B vs B2C mode with reverse charge guidance
  • US Sales Tax for all 50 states
  • A complete invoice generator with print and share

Why Free?

VATCalc is funded by Google AdSense advertising. This means we can offer all tools completely free, forever — no subscriptions, no paywalls, no "premium" features locked away.

We believe that tax calculation tools should be free and accessible to every business owner and freelancer in the world, regardless of their budget.

By keeping ads non-intrusive and clearly labeled, we maintain both a great user experience and a sustainable business model.

Our Commitment to Accuracy

Tax rates change frequently. A country might increase its standard rate, adjust reduced rates for essential goods, or introduce entirely new rate categories. We monitor official tax authority announcements across all covered countries and update our database when changes occur.

Sources we monitor include:

  • European Commission VAT database (EU countries)
  • OECD Consumption Tax Trends database
  • Individual country tax authority websites (IRS, HMRC, BZSt, DGFiP etc.)
  • KPMG and Deloitte global indirect tax guides
⚠️

Despite our best efforts, some rates may lag behind recent changes. Always verify with your local tax authority for compliance purposes. See our Disclaimer for more information.
