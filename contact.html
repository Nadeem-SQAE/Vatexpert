Frequently Asked Questions

Is VATCalc completely free?
Yes, all tools are permanently free. We're funded by Google AdSense ads.

How often are rates updated?
We monitor official tax authority announcements and update rates as soon as changes are announced.

Can I use the invoices for official purposes?
Invoice templates are provided as a starting point. Please verify local requirements and consult an accountant for official invoices.

Is my data private?
All calculations happen in your browser. We never store your amounts or invoice data. See our Privacy Policy.

Report an Incorrect Rate

Found a VAT rate that's out of date? We appreciate your help keeping our database accurate. Please include:

  • The country and product category affected
  • The rate currently shown on VATCalc
  • The correct rate
  • A link to the official source if possible

We'll review and update within 24 hours.
